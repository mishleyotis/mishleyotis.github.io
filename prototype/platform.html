Platform
Static walkthrough · no scripts

Where a platform genuinely fits, what to do first, and in what order.

01

Platform fit and story

Five platform tiles, each with its fit score, the gaps it addresses, and a story about this client rather than about the platform. The fit score is computed deterministically and the agent explains it; it never recomputes or re-ranks it, and the detail view has to agree with the headline.

02

Recommendations

What to do, why, what it depends on, the effort involved, and the capabilities it targets. Recommendations written by an analyst and recommendations synthesised by the system stay distinguishable from each other.

03

Conversation starters

The questions worth asking in the next meeting, derived from the findings rather than from a generic discovery script.

04

Transformation roadmap

Phased sequencing with each phase's capabilities, dependencies, and horizon. Phase order cannot contradict the prerequisites named in the recommendations.

05

Stair-step curve

The ladder from current maturity to target, step by step, with what each step unlocks. An absent ladder shows a stated empty state rather than an error.
