Artificial intelligence strategy for a brand launch

A small consumer business does not have an accounting team, a business intelligence team, and a content studio. This project asked what one person with the right workflow can replace, and where that stops being a good idea.

Role
Artificial intelligence strategy lead
Since
February 2026
Covers
Marketing, finance, and reporting
Launch month
1.5 thousand impressions, 1.2 thousand views
Finance

One workflow instead of two tools

Financial management, forecasting, and results dashboarding run through a single language-model workflow, consolidating what would otherwise need separate accounting and business intelligence software. For a business at this size, the overhead of two systems is the real cost.

Content

Launch material made in-house

Images, video, and design produced with multi-modal generative tools, on a schedule a launch actually needs rather than one an agency can accommodate. The constraint was consistency: a brand that looks different in every post does not read as a brand.

Judgement

Deciding where it does not belong

Defining where generative artificial intelligence adds value is mostly deciding where it does not. Pricing decisions, supplier relationships, and anything a customer would notice as generated stayed with people.

Results

1.5 thousand Instagram impressions and 1.2 thousand TikTok views in the launch month.

Modest numbers for a brand nobody had heard of, achieved without a content budget. The more useful outcome is that the owner can now see the month's finances and the month's reach in the same place.

This is the project where I get to be the client. It is a good corrective, because a consumer brand has no tolerance for a workflow that only works when the person who built it is available.
