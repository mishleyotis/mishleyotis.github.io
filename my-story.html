My story

I came to artificial intelligence through the audit floor.

I trained as an electrical and telecommunications engineer, then spent two years at EY turning manual audit work into automated pipelines. That is where the habit formed: before you automate something, you have to be able to say how you would prove it was right.

Artificial intelligence made that question urgent rather than academic. A model will produce a confident answer whether or not it has grounds for one.

So the work I do now is mostly about grounds: retrieval over curated knowledge instead of an opaque index, agents that challenge each other, and gates that are allowed to refuse a page rather than publish a weak one.

Financial services is the right place for that discipline. It is a regulated industry where a claim has to survive a risk committee, and nobody is impressed by a system that is usually right.

What I do

Design agent systems and artificial intelligence skills, build the applications they serve, deploy them on Google Cloud, and write the governance that keeps them trustworthy once other people depend on them.

How I work

Requirements first, written down. Then the technical design, then the interface brief, then the code. Every project on this site has those documents attached, and they are the working versions.
